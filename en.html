Privacy Policy – “Vodoemi BG”

Effective from 24 November 2026. Български

“Vodoemi BG” is a free app for anglers – a map of Bulgarian waters, fish species, navigation, comments and photos. This policy explains what data is processed, why, and what your rights are. The app shows no ads, uses no behavioural analytics services – only anonymous crash reports, and does not sell or share data with advertisers.

Data controller: the developer of the “Vodoemi BG” app, contact email: icaka0710@gmail.com.

1. No registration

No registration, email or phone number is required. When you first post a comment or photo, the app creates an anonymous account in Firebase Authentication (Google). It is a random identifier (UID) that contains no name, email or other personal information, and is used only so that you can delete your own content.

Sign in with Google (optional). If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the anonymous account is linked to your Google account. Firebase Authentication then stores the email, name and identifier of the Google account so that you can sign in again on another phone. Your email is not shown to other users. You can sign out at any time from “👤 Account & data”.

3. Legal basis

Posted content is processed on the basis of your consent (the “Post” / “Add photo” action) and to provide the service you requested. Location is used only after explicit permission in Android, which you can withdraw at any time in the phone settings.

4. External services

To work, the app connects to the following services. They receive the device's IP address, as with any web page:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage) – storing comments, photos and reports. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Play location services – determining the phone's location.
  • OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, OSM France – map images (tiles).
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) – the list of water bodies on manual refresh.
  • Google News (news.google.com) – the fishing news headlines on “Home”; receives only the search request (and your IP address), no data about you. Articles open on the publishers' sites.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons – photos of fish species and water bodies.
  • Google Maps (Places API) – only when you tap “🔎 More shops from Google Maps” in “📍 Waters near me”: the search area (your approximate location and the radius) is sent to find tackle shops. Results are not stored. Google privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) – the weather forecast; receives only the coordinates of the water bodies (also for the “Conditions” rating and river discharge). For “Weather where you are” on Home it receives your location rounded to about 10 km – not the exact one.
  • IARA (iara.government.bg) – news in the rules section.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) – reservoir levels from the Ministry of Environment bulletin; a shared file is downloaded, with no data about you.
  • Gmail / mail app – only for “Report poaching”, when you send the email yourself.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) – only if the app crashes: technical details of the error (the place in the code, phone model, Android and app version) and an anonymous installation identifier. No name, email, location, photos or comments. Kept for up to 90 days and used only to fix bugs. Firebase privacy.
  • Navigation app (e.g. Google Maps) – with “Navigate” it receives the water body's coordinates.
  • “📨 Invite a friend” – the link contains only the water's name and coordinates, the day and the time; you send it yourself (Viber, Messenger, etc.). It opens a page on this site (GitHub Pages) that stores nothing.
  • “📍 Send where I am” – your exact location at that moment is put into a text with a Google Maps link, which you send yourself to whoever you choose. The app does not store it or send it anywhere on its own.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Comments, photos and places are kept until deleted by you or by an administrator.
  • You can delete your own comment, photo or place with the delete button in the app.
  • Deleting your account and all data: in the app open 🗺️ → 👤 Account & data → 🗑️ Delete account and all data. The following are deleted immediately and permanently: all your comments and photos (including the files), reported catches and leaderboard entries, places you added, reports you submitted, the cloud catch log and its photos, the account itself, and all app data on the phone. Nothing is kept in a backup.
  • If the app is no longer installed, request deletion on the web page https://icaka0710-creator.github.io/vodoemibg-privacy/delete-account.html or by the contact email – we complete it within 30 days.
  • The anonymous account is tied to the installation. After uninstalling without Google sign-in the app cannot recognize your old content – in that case include the water body, date and text in your request.
  • Content reports are deleted after an administrator reviews them. Problem reports about the app (with any reply) are kept until the administrator deletes them or the reporter deletes their account.
  • Data on the phone is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and withdrawal of consent. To exercise your rights, write to icaka0710@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 14 to post content without a parent's consent. If you believe a child has posted personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Moderation and blocking

Every comment, photo and place can be reported (“🚩 Report”); an administrator reviews reports and removes inappropriate content. You can also block a user – their comments, photos, places and catches are then hidden on your phone. The block list is stored only on the phone and managed from “👤 Account & data”.

9. Security

Connections to all services are encrypted (HTTPS). The Firebase access rules allow deletion only by the author of the content or by an administrator.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address and in the app, with an updated date at the top.
