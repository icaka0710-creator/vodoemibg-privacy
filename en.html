Privacy Policy – “Vodoemi BG”

Effective from 18 November 2026. Български

“Vodoemi BG” is a free app for anglers – a map of Bulgarian waters, fish species, routes, comments and photos. This policy explains what data is processed, why, and what your rights are. The app shows no ads, uses no analytics services, and does not sell or share data with advertisers.

Data controller: the developer of the “Vodoemi BG” app, contact email: icaka0710@gmail.com.

1. No registration

No registration, email or phone number is required. When you first post a comment or photo, the app creates an anonymous account in Firebase Authentication (Google). It is a random identifier (UID) that contains no name, email or other personal information, and is used only so that you can delete your own content.

Sign in with Google (optional). If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the anonymous account is linked to your Google account. Firebase Authentication then stores the email, name and identifier of the Google account so that you can sign in again on another phone. Your email is not shown to other users. You can sign out at any time from “👤 Account”.

3. Legal basis

Posted content is processed on the basis of your consent (the “Post” / “Add photo” action) and to provide the service you requested. Location is used only after explicit permission in Android, which you can withdraw at any time in the phone settings.

4. External services

To work, the app connects to the following services. They receive the device's IP address, as with any web page:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage) – storing comments, photos and reports. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Play location services – determining the phone's location.
  • OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, OSM France – map images (tiles).
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) – the list of water bodies on manual refresh.
  • OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) – route calculation; receives the start and end point only when you tap “Route on map”.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons – fish species photos.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) – the weather forecast; receives only the coordinates of the selected water body (not your location).
  • IARA (iara.government.bg) – news in the rules section.
  • Navigation app (e.g. Google Maps) – with “Navigate” it receives the water body's coordinates.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Comments and photos are kept until deleted by you or by an administrator.
  • You can delete your own comment or photo with the delete button in the app.
  • The anonymous account is tied to the installation. After uninstalling, the app cannot recognize your old content – in that case write to the contact email with the water body, date and text, and we will delete it. If you signed in with Google, just sign in again.
  • Reports are deleted after an administrator reviews them.
  • Data on the phone is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and withdrawal of consent. To exercise your rights, write to icaka0710@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 14 to post content without a parent's consent. If you believe a child has posted personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to all services are encrypted (HTTPS). The Firebase access rules allow deletion only by the author of the content or by an administrator.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address and in the app, with an updated date at the top.
